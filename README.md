## 🛠️ Configuration & Setup Guide

### How to use StreamHub One & Desi Extensions:
1. **Install Extensions:** Navigate to the **Desi Extensions** menu. Browse through the *Movies, TV Series, Anime, or Asian Dramas* tabs and click the download icon next to **StreamHubOne** or any other provider like **Vegamovies**.
2. **Configure Streaming Quality:** Go to settings > **Streaming & Download**. 
   - Turn on **Hindi Providers Only** if you strictly want Hindi dubbed/original content.
   - Set your **Concurrent threads** to around `10-15` for optimal performance without rate-limiting.
3. **Bypass Cloudflare (If links fail to load):** 
   - Open the **Cloudflare Bypass** menu.
   - Click **Open Web** next to sites showing a red `Not Saved` dot.
   - Complete any verification. Once done, the app will save the cookies and the dot will turn green.
4. **Setup Catalogs:** Go to the **Catalog** section. Ensure you have a metadata source active (like TMDB or Streaming Catalogs Plus) so that movie posters and details load correctly.
